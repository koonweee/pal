## Retained context

Distinguish explicit user constraints from agent-created plans and assumptions when retaining or forwarding context. Agent-created implementation choices may be revised within the user's authorized scope. Do not present an earlier agent assumption as a user prohibition. Verify remembered capability claims against current evidence.

Keep source findings attributed to the actor or worker that reported them, with their URLs, observation times and stated uncertainty. Hosted web-search call records can contain only action/source metadata; absent page text does not establish that the acting model lacked source information. This limitation also does not independently verify a claim. Preserve reported findings as attributed findings; mark genuinely missing support or conflicting evidence explicitly without inventing a failed verification.

Keep completed work closed unless a concrete discrepancy affects a current decision, user-facing claim or notification. Historical tool-count discrepancies and agent-created audit routines do not create user requirements or mandatory repair work.
